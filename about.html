On the floor

The loom behind every design

Our sarees are woven on a triple pana rapier jacquard machine — a loom that can weave three widths of fabric side by side while executing the intricate booti, border, and pallu patterns that define brocade work. It's this machine that gives our designs the raised zari texture visible up close.

Raw material

The yarn behind the colourways

Every colourway starts here — cones of silk and zari filament, dyed and staged before they reach the loom. The teal, olive, gold and coral tones in our current line all trace back to this shelf.
